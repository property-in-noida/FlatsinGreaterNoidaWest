When Can Under-Construction Flats Cost More Than Ready Homes?

Although under-construction properties are often expected to be more affordable, this is not always the case. Newer residential developments, especially in Sector 12, Greater Noida West, can sometimes command higher prices than completed homes because of their specifications, amenities and market demand. Several factors can contribute to this price difference:

  • Modern specifications: Contemporary layouts, fittings and building designs.
  • Lifestyle facilities: Clubhouses, landscaped areas and recreational amenities.
  • Location advantages: Proximity to employment hubs, established neighbourhoods and major roads.
  • Buyer demand: Interest in newer residential communities can support premium pricing.

For buyers considering flats in Noida Extension, comparing property age, condition and facilities is equally important. Older completed apartments may have lower asking prices but require renovation.

What Should You Check Before Buying a Flat?

When comparing flats in Noida Extension, take time to review the important details and verify all information. Ask questions from your agent or developer to understand how practical the home is, its future ROI and whether it would support your changing lifestyle.

Use the following list to ensure you have covered the essential checks before making your purchase decision.

  1. Verify project registration on the UP-RERA portal.
  2. Check the occupancy certificate for completed properties.
  3. Review ownership documents and sanctioned plans.
  4. Compare carpet areas.
  5. Confirm maintenance and parking charges in writing.
  6. Check construction progress and the developer's delivery record.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are ready-to-move flats cheaper than under-construction flats?

Not necessarily. September 2026 listing averages showed ready homes at ₹8,016 per sq. ft. and under-construction properties at ₹8,600 per sq. ft.

2. Is GST applicable on ready-to-move flats?

Generally, GST does not apply when the entire consideration is received after the completion certificate or first occupation, whichever is earlier.

3. Is buying an under-construction flat risky?

There are construction and possession risks. Check UP-RERA registration, approvals and the developer's delivery record.

4. Can you negotiate the price of a ready-to-move flat?

Yes. Property condition, resale urgency and prevailing market rates can influence negotiations.

5. What is more important, price per sq. ft. or carpet area?

Both matter, but carpet area indicates the actual usable space you are purchasing.
